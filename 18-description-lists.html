Words of the Web

HTML
The language that gives a web page its structure.
CSS
The language that gives a web page its colours and layout.
Browser
A program that shows web pages, for example Chrome or Firefox.
URL
The address of a page on the Web.

Nigerian food

Suya
Spicy grilled meat on a stick.
It comes from the north of Nigeria and is loved everywhere.
Akara
Kosai
Fried bean cake. It is called akara in Yoruba and kosai in Hausa.
Garri
Dried, grated cassava. You can drink it with cold water or make eba.
