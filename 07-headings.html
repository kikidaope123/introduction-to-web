A short guide to Nigeria

The land

Nigeria is in West Africa. It has 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory.

Rivers

The two great rivers, the Niger and the Benue, meet at Lokoja.

Cities

Lagos is the largest city. Abuja is the capital.

The people

More than 200 million people live in Nigeria.

Languages

English is the official language. Hundreds of other languages are spoken.
