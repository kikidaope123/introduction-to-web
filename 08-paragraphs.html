Jollof rice is the most famous dish in West Africa.

It is made with rice, tomatoes, peppers, onions and spices.

Nigerians and Ghanaians have argued for many years about who cooks the best jollof rice. The friendly argument even has a name: the Jollof Wars. Each country is sure that its own recipe is the best, and nobody is ready to give up.
